Om Agencies collects customer phone numbers, names, and locality details strictly for rooftop solar feasibility assessments, quotation generation, and MSEDCL net-metering portal registration. We do not sell, rent, or distribute consumer data to third-party marketing brokers or lead-generation syndicates.

2. Benchmark Estimates vs. Final Site Survey Pricing

Published system prices and calculator estimates are computed based on standard ground or flat concrete RCC rooftop parameters with standard cable runs (up to 25 meters). Final commercial proposals may vary slightly based on structural elevation requirements (e.g., 10-ft high gazebos), heavy-gauge GI piping, or specialized earthing pits necessitated by local soil resistivity.

3. PM Surya Ghar Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Guidelines

The central government subsidy (₹30,000 to ₹78,000) under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is disbursed directly by the Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) into the consumer's Aadhaar-linked bank account following MSEDCL bidirectional meter installation and portal commissioning verification. Om Agencies facilitates all technical approvals and documentation without charging additional processing surcharges.
